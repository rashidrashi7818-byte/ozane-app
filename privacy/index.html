Ozane Accounts — Privacy Policy

Tayba Perfumes · Last updated 22 September 2026

Ozane Accounts is an internal app for the owners and staff of Tayba Perfumes, including the Ozane brand. It records the business's own purchases, stock, sales, expenses and cash at our outlets. It is not a shop and it is not for the general public: logins are created by the business owner, and nobody can sign themselves up.

What the app stores

  • Login details. The work email address of each user, their password in encrypted form, the outlets they are assigned to, and whether their access is switched on. Passwords are handled by our database provider and are never shown to the business or to the app.
  • The business records a user enters. Supplier bills, payments and deliveries, stock counts and transfers, sales and refunds, expenses and cash counts. Each record keeps the account that saved it and the time it was saved, so the business can see who entered what.
  • No customer names, phone numbers, card numbers or bank details are collected.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no profiling.
  • No access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, files or calls. The app asks for internet access only.
  • Data is never sold, and it is not shared with anyone outside the business apart from the hosting provider described below.

Where the data is kept

The data is held in the business's own database, hosted by Supabase on servers in Singapore. Everything the app sends travels over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Rules inside the database limit each staff member to the outlets they are assigned; only the owner can see every outlet, the reports and the staff settings.

How long it is kept

Business records are kept for as long as the business needs them for its accounts and tax records. The owner can switch off a staff member's access at any time, which stops that login working immediately.

Your choices

  • Staff can change their own password in the app, under My account.
  • To have a login and the email address in it removed, write to the address below. The business entries already recorded stay in the accounts, as bookkeeping requires, identified only by an internal reference.

Children

The app is for people who work for the business. It is not directed at children and is not offered to them.

Changes

If the app changes what it stores, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.
