On Android

Download for Android 4 MB · Android 6.0 or newer
  1. Tap the button above. Your phone saves the file to Downloads.
  2. Open it. Android asks whether to allow installing apps from wherever you opened it (Chrome, WhatsApp, Files) — allow it once.
  3. Tap Install, then open TAYBA and sign in with the login you were given.

Already have the app? Install this straight over it. You stay signed in and nothing is lost — only the name and icon change. Do not uninstall the old one first.

On iPhone or iPad

There is nothing to download. You add TAYBA to your home screen instead, and it opens like any other app.

  1. Open this page in Safari. It must be Safari — Chrome on iPhone cannot do the next step.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen).
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open TAYBA from your home screen and sign in with the login you were given.

Add it to the home screen — do not just bookmark it. A bookmark signs you out after a week of not using it. The home-screen version keeps you signed in.

This app is for Tayba Perfumes staff. It needs an internet connection and your own login; there is nothing to see without one.
