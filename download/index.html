Put the icon on your phone

Android. Open TAYBA with the button above. Under the sign-in box there is a button that says Install TAYBA on this phone — tap it, then tap Install. No warnings, nothing to download.

Installed it and cannot see it? Android puts new apps in the apps list, not always on the home screen. Swipe up from the bottom of the home screen to see every app and look for TAYBA there. Press and hold it to drag it onto your home screen. It can take a few seconds to appear.

iPhone. Open TAYBA in Safari (not Chrome). Tap the Share button at the bottom — the square with an arrow coming out of it — scroll down the list, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Add it to the home screen — do not just bookmark it. A bookmark signs you out after a week of not using it. The home-screen version keeps you signed in.

Or install the Android file

You do not need this. It is the same app. Use it only if you want TAYBA to work from the app drawer like a shop-bought app.

Download for Android 4 MB · Android 7 or newer

Your phone will show warnings on the way. That is normal — it is because the file comes from us and not from the Play Store. Three screens stop most people, and on each one the way forward is the small grey writing, not the big blue button:

  1. “This type of file can harm your device” → tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the file. If it says “not allowed to install unknown apps” → tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, press Back.
  3. If it says “Unsafe app blocked” → tap More details, then Install anyway.

Then tap Install, wait, and Open.

If it still will not install: “App not installed” means an older copy is on the phone — uninstall that first. Stuck on “Installing…” usually means the phone is out of space. Either way, the button at the top of this page works without any of this.

Already have the app? Install this straight over it. You stay signed in and nothing is lost. Do not uninstall the old one first.
